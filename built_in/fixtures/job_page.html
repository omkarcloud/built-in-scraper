The Role
Conduct statistical analysis, evaluate product experiments, analyze user interaction data, identify growth opportunities, and build dashboards, notebooks, or AI agents. Partner with data science, engineering, and business teams to communicate actionable insights. The internship requires full-time participation for 12 weeks in the Seattle hub and enrollment in a master's program with graduation by August 2028.

Skills Required

  • Proficiency with SQL or other query languages
  • Experience with Python or R for statistical computing, data analysis, and predictive modeling
  • Understanding of statistical methods, hypothesis testing, experimental design, A/B testing, or machine learning fundamentals
  • Experience with data visualization tools such as Tableau, Databricks, Mode, or other BI platforms
  • Familiarity with modern AI tools and generative AI workflows, such as LLM APIs, AI coding assistants, prompt engineering, or AI agents
  • Strong communication skills for conveying quantitative insights to technical and non-technical teammates
  • Availability for a 12-week full-time, 40-hour-per-week Summer 2027 internship
  • Currently enrolled full-time in a master's degree program and returning after the internship
  • Graduation by August 2028
  • Ability to work from the Seattle hub

What We Do

Atlassian creates teamwork solutions for high-performing teams. Our portfolio of collaboration and work management software products includes Jira, Confluence, Trello, Loom and Rovo. More than 300,000 businesses worldwide rely on Atlassian’s technology, including 80 percent of Fortune 500 companies. Our solutions support various business teams and they help organizations plan, track, and deliver their biggest ideas together.

Why Work With Us

At Atlassian, we believe we can accomplish so much more together than apart — which is why everything from our tooling — to our distributed workforce — to how our teams are structured is rooted in collaboration. Come join us and help unleash the potential of every team.
